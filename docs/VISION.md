# SilentChair — Product Vision

> Last updated: 2026-06-19

---

## What Is SilentChair?

SilentChair is the place people go to start and grow their businesses.

It is a SaaS platform that gives every user a full AI workforce — researchers, marketers, salespeople, customer service agents, financial advisors, business strategists, and more — that operates their business continuously on their behalf. Users describe a business they want to build, SilentChair's AI asks the right questions to deeply understand it, and then puts a team of AI employees to work: creating content, running campaigns, handling customers, chasing leads, managing budgets, and growing the company — without requiring the user to be an expert in any of these areas.

The goal is not to automate a single task. It is to give every entrepreneur — from a first-time founder to a seasoned operator — the equivalent of a full professional team, at a fraction of the cost.

---

## The Problem

Starting and running a business is expensive and overwhelming. Most people with a good business idea cannot afford the team they need to execute it. They wear every hat — marketer, salesperson, customer service rep, accountant, strategist — and they wear them badly, because no one is an expert in everything.

AI tools have become powerful enough to perform many of these functions at a professional level. But they exist as dozens of disconnected products that require technical expertise to configure and maintain, and none of them know anything about your specific business.

SilentChair solves both problems. A single platform. A team that already knows your business. Working for you around the clock.

## Who It's For

SilentChair is built for:

- First-time entrepreneurs who need guidance on everything
- Solo operators stretched too thin to grow
- Small business owners (1–20 employees) who can't afford specialists
- Side-project founders who want to move fast without hiring
- Anyone with a business idea and no idea where to start

SilentChair works for any language, any industry, any geography.

## Core Value Proposition

**"Hire a team. Run your business. We handle the rest."**

- **AI employees, not AI tools.** Users don't configure prompts or learn new software. They hire AI people — agents with names, roles, and expertise — and those people do the work.
- **The business is the center.** Every agent shares a deep, living understanding of the user's business. They all know the brand, the goals, the customers, and what's happening right now.
- **Your call on autonomy.** For every type of action an agent takes, the user chooses: let the AI handle it fully within budget limits, or require approval first. This is configurable per department, per action type, and can be changed anytime.
- **A real human in your corner.** Every user gets access to a human business coach who helps them build their business and get the most from the platform. Coaching sessions are scheduled from inside the app and the AI prepares the coach before every meeting.
- **Speak your language.** The platform detects the user's language and operates in it — the UI, the agents, and all generated content.

## The Living Business Document

At the core of every business on SilentChair is a **Living Business Document** — an AI-maintained profile that captures and continuously updates everything known about that business:

- What the business does and who it serves
- Brand voice, tone, and visual identity
- Products and services (descriptions, pricing, availability)
- Target customer profiles and key market insights
- Current goals and strategic priorities
- Competitive landscape
- Active campaigns, pipeline status, and open tasks
- Financial context: budget, expenses, revenue targets
- Key decisions, pivots, and milestones over time

This document is updated automatically as the business evolves — when the user announces a new product, changes their pricing, wins their first client, or shifts their direction. It is the shared context every AI agent operates from. All agents read from it; key developments update it.

Users see a plain-language summary of this document on their business dashboard. They can correct or update anything by simply telling the AI — "actually, our target customer is X not Y" — and the document is updated accordingly.

## The AI Workforce

SilentChair provides AI employees organized into departments. Each is a named AI persona with a defined role, personality, and set of tools. They operate independently but share the same business context, so a sales agent knows what the marketing agent just published, and the financial advisor knows what the advertising agent just spent.

### Departments

**Marketing**
Knows the brand deeply. Creates and schedules social media content across platforms, writes and publishes blog posts, designs and sends email campaigns, tracks performance, and adjusts based on results. Uses multiple image and video generation tools, selecting the best for each job.

**Sales**
Understands the user's products and services. Creates and executes outbound campaigns via email, SMS, and phone. Manages inbound leads through website chat and inbound calls. Maintains a pipeline and follows up consistently.

**Advertising**
Creates and manages paid campaigns on Facebook, Instagram, Google, and TikTok. Generates ad creative — copy, images, and video — sets up targeting, manages spend within budget limits, monitors performance, and optimizes. Can operate fully autonomously within budget or present campaigns for approval before launch.

**Customer Service**
Handles inbound customer queries through website chat, email, SMS, and phone. Resolves issues, answers product questions, processes returns, and escalates to the user when genuinely needed. Trained on the user's products, policies, and tone.

**Client Acquisition**
Researches potential clients using the methods appropriate for the user's industry. Executes targeted outreach. Manages follow-up. Tracks pipeline. Knows when to hand off to Sales.

**Financial Advisor**
Helps the user understand and organize their finances. Plans and tracks budgets, models cash flow, flags overspending or risk, and helps the user prepare for financial decisions. Works from data the user provides and data the platform tracks internally.

**Business Advisor**
The strategic voice. Monitors business performance, keeps up with relevant trends, challenges assumptions, surfaces opportunities, and proactively suggests improvements. Acts as a thinking partner, not just an executor.

### How Agents Coordinate

Agents do not operate in silos. They share the Living Business Document as common ground. The Business Advisor can prompt the Marketing team to shift strategy. The Financial Advisor can flag when the Advertising agent is approaching budget limits. The Sales agent can inform the Customer Service agent about a specific deal that just closed. This coordination happens through shared context and structured handoffs — not separate human instructions.

### The Workforce Over Time

As SilentChair grows, the workforce expands. Future departments include HR and Recruitment, Legal, and Product. The Persona Marketplace (see below) will allow third-party creators to contribute additional specialists the platform doesn't build itself.

## Human Coaching

Every SilentChair user gets access to a real human business coach included in their subscription.

Coaches are not a support team. They are business professionals who help users:
1. Build and grow their specific business
2. Get the most out of the SilentChair platform

**How it works:**
- Users schedule sessions from inside the app using integrated calendar booking (Calendly or equivalent)
- Sessions are conducted via video call (Zoom or equivalent)
- Before every session, the AI prepares the coach with a full briefing: the state of the business, what the AI workforce has done since the last session, current goals and blockers, and suggested topics
- After the session, the user's stated decisions and directions are captured and used to update the Living Business Document

**Coaching tiers:**
- Starter: 1 async coaching session/month (text-based, coach responds within 48 hours)
- Growth: 1 video session/month
- Scale: 2 video sessions/month
- Enterprise: Custom

The distinction at Starter is practical — video coaching at the lowest tier would make the tier unprofitable. Async text coaching is still high-value and allows more people access to real human guidance.

## Subscription Model

SilentChair uses a **dollar balance model**. Each subscription tier includes a monthly AI spend allowance — a real dollar amount that decreases as agents do work. Users can see exactly what each task cost them after it completes. This keeps the system honest: as AI models change or improve, pricing reflects actual cost rather than arbitrary credit conversions.

### How It Works

- Each subscription tier includes a monthly AI spend balance (e.g., $30/month of AI spend included in Starter)
- Every agent action deducts its actual cost — with a platform markup — from the balance
- After each task completes, the user sees what it cost (retrospective transparency)
- Future: show estimated cost before approving an action
- When the balance runs low, the user tops up in dollar blocks (no abstraction, no conversion)
- There is no limit on the number of businesses. Users can run as many businesses as they like — more businesses just consume more of their AI spend balance

### Tiers

| Tier | Subscription | Included AI Spend | Coaching |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $99/mo | $30/mo AI spend | 1 async/mo |
| Growth | $249/mo | $100/mo AI spend | 1 video/mo |
| Scale | $599/mo | $300/mo AI spend | 2 video/mo |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Custom |

**AI spend top-ups:** Available in any amount ($10 minimum). Unused balance rolls over for 60 days.

**Notes:**
- All plans include all departments and unlimited businesses — tier differences are AI spend budget and coaching volume only
- Platform markup on AI costs covers infrastructure, support, and platform development — exact margins to be set before launch
- Pricing should be validated with a financial model before public launch — the above is a starting point
- A 14-day free trial with $5 in AI spend included, no credit card required

## Language and Internationalization

SilentChair detects the user's system language on first use and operates in it by default — the UI, agent conversations, and all generated content.

A **language selector button is always visible in the navigation bar on every page** — no need to go to settings. One click changes the language immediately for the entire platform. The setting is also available in the user profile for discoverability.

AI agents are capable of operating in any language the underlying models support. Region-appropriate content (ad targeting, social platforms, business norms) is factored in by agents when the business context indicates a specific geography.

## The Persona Marketplace (Future)

SilentChair will open a **Persona Marketplace** allowing third-party creators to build and sell AI employee personas.

- Creators define a persona using a structured file format (prompt file + metadata + tool configuration)
- Every submission goes through manual review and approval before listing
- Community ratings surface quality over time
- Creators set their own price; SilentChair takes a platform cut
- Built-in SilentChair personas are always free to all users

This allows the workforce to expand into highly specialized verticals — niche industries, specific compliance environments, regional expertise — without SilentChair having to build every variation internally.

## The Talent Network (Future)

SilentChair will eventually operate as a **temp agency and recruitment platform**, connecting users with:

- Human contractors and freelancers for tasks AI cannot handle (physical, legal, highly specialized)
- Full-time hire candidates for users who are ready to grow their human team
- Vetted partner companies for specialized services

The AI workforce surfaces these needs proactively — "You've hit a point where a human graphic designer might serve you better than AI generation for your brand assets. Here are three options."

## Scale and Architecture Philosophy

SilentChair is designed from day one to serve tens of thousands of concurrent businesses without degradation.

Key principles:

- **Async by default.** Agent work runs in background task queues, not in the user's request cycle. Users see results when ready; the app never blocks.
- **Isolated per business.** Each business's data, agent context, task queue, and spending limits are fully isolated from every other business.
- **Predictable cost.** The credit system caps what any user can spend the platform's AI budget on. No runaway costs from a single user.
- **Multi-model intelligence.** The platform selects the right AI model for each task — Claude for deep reasoning and writing, specialized models for image, video, and voice — optimizing for quality and cost at each step.
- **Stateful agents.** The Living Business Document gives every agent persistent, evolving context. Agents don't start from scratch on every run.
- **Built for compliance.** As the platform grows and users store sensitive data (client lists, financial info, employee records), the architecture will support GDPR, CCPA, and eventually SOC 2.
